## 🧑‍🎓 About Me

<img align="right" width="36%" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NishankKS/NishankKS/main/assets/about.gif" alt="" />

Hi, I'm **Nishank** 👋 I build intelligent systems end to end: training the models, wiring them into services that hold up in production, and measuring where they break before anyone else has to.

- 🎓 **M.Sc. Data Science** at **TU Braunschweig**, specialising in **LLMs, Retrieval-Augmented Generation and Agentic AI**
- 🧠 **B.E. in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning**, Dayananda Sagar College of Engineering, Bengaluru
- 📄 **3 peer-reviewed publications** in IEEE and Springer venues, Scopus indexed
- 🏆 **3× hackathon winner / runner-up** across national and inter-college competitions
- 📍 Based in **Braunschweig, Germany**
- 🎯 Open to **AI Engineer, ML Engineer and Data Scientist** roles across Germany and Europe
